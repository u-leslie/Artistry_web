# Artistry 💕

**Where photography meets poetry.**

Artistry is a small corner of the internet where I get to share poems, photographs,<br>
thoughts, and little moments that mean something to me.

I created it as a way to hold onto the things I feel, the things I notice, and the things<br>
I sometimes can't quite put into words.

**It’s more than a project to me. It’s a piece very close to my heart, and one of the most<br>
personal things I’ve ever created. ❤️**

Thank you for being here and taking a little time to experience it.

*— Leslie* 🤍
